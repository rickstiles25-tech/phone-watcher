For the best reads

  • Keep the phone still: lean it on something. It only reads once the picture settles.
  • Frame the monitor using the zoom slider (supports wide-angle < 1.0× if your phone hardware allows, or 1.0×–4.0×).
  • Angle the phone a little to dodge glare. Keep it on a charger for long sessions.
